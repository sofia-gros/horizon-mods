# Jeweled Apple acquisition

The pinned TConstruct `f4e77d43` table recipe consumes an apple and 200 mB molten diamond and cools for 118 ticks. It is already in the packaged original recipe archive; this change registers its previously missing result and source artwork. Two diamonds each melt into 100 mB at 1450°C. The bounded stock piglin barter route can supply Blazing Blood for high-temperature fuel; source entity melting and Blaze Head melting are separate work.

The apple is always edible, supplies 4 nutrition and 9.6 saturation, and applies Haste I for 1200 ticks and Resistance I for 2400 ticks. The native content declaration specifies those components. The desktop manual overlay leaves this item to the later content preparation step to avoid shifting existing manual-overlay protocol IDs. The pinned recipe and lookup test use the source cast, fluid amount, result, consumed-cast flag and cooling time. The result is not a claim that its separate modifier recipe works, or that Cheese Ingot's effect removal is complete.

Scoped data and desktop resource tests do not prove the live registry, actual casting, eating, effect synchronization, Creative placement, save/reload, custom data packs, integrated host build, or LAN behavior. Earlier bounded lookup tests are not a full host or live casting test. Native preparation may reassign numeric item IDs; ordinary NBT stacks are name-based but cross-version and LAN behavior remain unverified.
