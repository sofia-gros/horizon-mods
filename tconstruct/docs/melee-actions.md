# Source melee area actions

This draft implements source circle attacks for Scythe and Kama, source sweep
attacks for Sword and Cleaver, and the built-in Knockback levels of Excavator
and Battlesign. It builds on the material damage and durability paths already
present. Circle and sweep have distinct eligibility and damage rules; neither
is considered implemented merely because the equipment is registered.

Acceptance requires representative targets inside and outside the source
ranges, source charge/ground/sprint conditions, no duplicate primary hit,
allied and ineligible target exclusions, attack damage and knockback, tool wear
and breakage, hosted LAN and player permissions, plus a vanilla sword
regression. Windows and all individual material combinations are not assumed
verified. Expanded, off-hand attack, other modifier abilities, armor and ranged
weapons remain subsequent work. Crop shears loot remains unimplemented.
