# Foundry

The Foundry is in development. It will use a scorched multiblock structure and liquid fuel to melt materials and collect ore byproducts.

The first playable setup will melt a representative ore, retain its primary fluid and byproduct, and pour a selected fluid through a faucet into a casting table or basin. The controller screen, saved state and LAN use are part of this work. These operations are not implemented or verified yet.

The Foundry uses its own structure, capacity and fuel rules. Other specialized recipes and automation remain separate work.
