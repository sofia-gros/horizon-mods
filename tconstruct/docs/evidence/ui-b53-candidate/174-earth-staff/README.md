# Earth Staff stat-based repair

Same combined candidate, client SHA, #263 content and isolated corrections as [the Flint and Brick fixture](../174-flint-and-brick/README.md). Not an unmodified exact-head build.

Fresh creative fixture: a damage-400 Earth Staff (max damage 800) with no material-part list rejected dirt in the Tinker Station and kept both inputs. Replacing dirt with Greenheart Planks produced a staff with damage 275. The source staff definition references slimewood limb stats and the Greenheart Planks source material recipe resolves a slimewood variant. The screenshots are actual client captures. The adapted script/log label `BACON` is historical; the actual input is `tconstruct:greenheart_planks`. Other staff variants and independent #174 gates remain open.
