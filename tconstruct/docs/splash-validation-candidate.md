## Conditions before a production recipe

Do not enable the normal Station recipe merely to make the diagnostic reachable. First establish the integrated apply/build and live behavior above, source recipe ingredient and slot consumption, guest/host synchronization, and correct handling of supported and unsupported source fluids, targets, reach, priority, partial fluid and area effects. The present implementation deliberately holds local non-integrated-host targets, partially funded groups with unknown entity query order, special Mob/armor-stand cases and multiple original entity/block effects; box/circle/vein resource guards and unloaded-chunk behavior are not source limits. Those gaps need implementation or an explicit product-scope decision before claiming full source parity or enabling a broadly applicable recipe.
