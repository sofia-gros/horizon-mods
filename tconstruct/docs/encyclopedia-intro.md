# Encyclopedia introduction

The pinned TConstruct recipe casts a book with 90 mB molten gold over 57 ticks. The source recipe is already in the bundled original pack. This candidate registers the book, its source artwork, and its two source introduction pages. A prominent notice precedes them: material lists, tools, modifiers and fluid effects are not yet included, and the original foreword describes the complete book rather than the present port. Only the introductory chapter is enabled.

Material and modifier chapters require their own source-compatible data selection and rendering; this is not an implementation of the whole Encyclopedia. Gameplay casting, actual reading and rendering, guest synchronization, resource-pack overrides, language changes and host integration remain unverified. The normal book reader and packet paths are reused; unsupported chapters are not advertised as present.
