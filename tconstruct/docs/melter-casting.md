# Melting and casting

The melter and casting setup is under development. The first playable setup will melt supported inputs using fuel, transfer the liquid through a faucet, and cast a finished item on a casting table or in a basin.

This unit includes the blocks, controls, recipes, fluid transfer, and saved state needed for that sequence. The larger Smeltery, Foundry, and alloying are separate work. The setup is not available to play until the full sequence has been checked in the game.
