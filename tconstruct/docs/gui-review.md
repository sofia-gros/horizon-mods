# Existing-screen GUI checklist

The reference is the fixed Tinkers' Construct source and its original assets. These checks describe the implemented screens; representative checks are not a claim of pixel-perfect parity in every state or language.

| Screen | Source-backed paths and representative checks | Known gaps or checks still needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Part Builder | Source background, pattern grid, item slots, information panel and side-inventory layout; shared drawing and hit positions. A chest input, part result and save/reload passed on Linux; chest and hopper scrolling have representative earlier checks. | Named adjacent chests display their custom text in normal and LAN games. The representative chest was checked at 960 × 540; all custom text styles and storage variants were not individually tested. |
| Tinker Station | Source layout coordinates, result location, tab buttons, equipment/offhand slots, name input and information panels. Result rendering and clicks passed at both sizes; representative tab and equipment interactions passed locally and over LAN. | Dormant input visibility, insertion rejection, ordinary removal and Shift removal passed normal and LAN checks. An armor stand with a crafted dagger was visually checked at 960 × 540 and 1280 × 720; drag rotation and a helmet layer were checked in the game; exact armor-layer alignment against an original screenshot remains unverified. The translucent repair background is the original enlarged repair icon and ITEM_COVER overlay, not stray rectangles. |
| Melter | Source background, item and fuel slots, fluid and heat bars, tank/fuel tooltips and held-bucket clicks share the machine code. Liquid-fuel melting and bucket transfers passed locally and over LAN; the Lava temperature tooltip was checked in game. | More source fuel and heat status states and the second screen size need representative checks. Source comments claim empty-space left click inserts a container, but the fixed revision's `ClickableTankModule.tryClick` and `MelterContainerMenu.clickMenuButton` send/interpret left as fill and right as empty even in empty space; the implementation follows that executable source behavior. |
| Smeltery and Foundry | Source background, slots, fluid layers, heat/fuel indicator, capacity and mode tooltips, scrolling and item hit positions use shared code. Fuel and copper heat displays were seen in the Linux game, including a LAN guest; a Foundry capacity tooltip was seen. | Right-click fluid injection and fuel-gauge bucket transfers passed representative normal and LAN tests. The heating panel, item and heat bar alignment were visually checked at 960 × 540 and 1280 × 720. Pagination/scrolling and every heat/tooltip state are not individually verified. |

Only Part Builder and Tinker Station tabs currently open implemented workstation menus; other registered workstations have no corresponding GUI and are shown as unavailable. Unsupported machine recipes, modifiers and blocks are not implied by the GUI. Large multi-viewer combinations, every item/material, all languages and Windows have not been individually verified.

The representative [heating panel](smeltery-heating-alignment.png), [Station preview](station-preview-960.png) and [named storage](builder-named-storage.png) screenshots are from the Linux game.
