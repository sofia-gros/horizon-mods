# Connected glass

Tinkers' Construct connected glass and panes are not available yet. This work adds their original textures and connected appearance to Horizon.

The implementation needs to select each face's texture from its neighbors, load the required sprites, apply the original colors and transparency, and preserve pane connections and collision. Glass must be available through the Creative inventory and work when placed, broken, saved, and used in multiplayer.

Smeltery behavior, material-dependent anvils, and custom storage are separate work. The connected appearance is not complete until it has been checked in the game at boundaries and with different neighboring blocks.
