## Exact ordering and the intentional difference

The original complete actual plan has six handle_packet HEADs; precisely broad,
haste and materials-book-response cancel. Their actual target node is [44,68]
with fingerprint 9f16b281b32524d06842ac401582ce22a2c022e4b8dc45a43bfc82bd98eb9724.
The recovered plan hash and exact helper inventory are recorded in inputs.json.

The candidate order is:

1. The existing HEADs run in their planned order. Broad/Haste keep their state
   write at HEAD; Guide only ensures its static registration here.
2. handle_packet calls native_client_packet as its first native statement.
3. The native observer body runs. Its sole Pluto RETURN snapshots handlers,
   performs read-only payload observation, then re-reads sequence/current
   non-lectern View and applies the trusted private Guide update once.
4. The remaining native handle_packet match runs, then returns.

The Guide update is therefore after native_client_packet, NOT after the entire
handle_packet body. The four native CustomPayload branches use horizon:mod_net,
horizon:attachment_sync, horizon:menu_extra and horizon:recipe_content. All are
disjoint from the three TConstruct channels; those reach the native no-op
wildcard. Full source hashes and channel declarations pin this statement.

The other HEADs handle menu/container packets. The smeltery HEAD can perform
additional read-only menu/world queries before its custom-payload early return;
that execution was formerly skipped by an earlier cancelling HEAD. No mutation
for the three custom channels was found in those exact sources. Restoring the
native mod observer and Pluto read-only subscribers is an intentional observable
change, not a claim of identical callback counts. Subscriber side effects or
panics are not rolled back. The complete-plan guard must retain sole RETURN,
exact source/placement/owner, and rejection of cancellable/interior caller hooks.
