The custom-data network adapters derive from `network-custom-data.rs` and
`client-network-custom-data.rs` in this repository's Tinkers port at commit
`37c780c0d968756d5f6a0213b2f0fa798219b930`. They retain the generic custom-data
transport and omit Tinkers-specific behavior. No third-party Tinkers assets are
included in this bundle.
